Problem — the system's most safety-critical transition has no owning module: four files re-derive how to find and flip the txn, their custody-ref formats actively disagree (escalated deposits silently match nothing), the dispute-pause check and impact tail are duplicated, and the HTTP seam lets any Individual post all-passing signals to verify their own credits.

Solution — deepen a single CreditVerification module that owns minting, verifying, rejecting and compensating ledger rows, generates and parses custody refs internally, and assembles gate signals from server-side facts; deposit, handover and redemption flows call it instead of re-assembling the flip.

  • locality: ledger state machine in one module
  • kills the CUSTODY-DEP- ref mismatch by construction
  • closes the client-side trust-bypass hole
  • tests stop hand-seeding creditTxns + trustDecisions
  • interface: 4 verbs replaces ~25 optional fields
  • leverage: 3 custody flows, 1 interface
