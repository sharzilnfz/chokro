5. Architectural Invariants & CLI Commands

1. Measurement Invariant: APPLIANCES, E_WASTE $\to$ unit: 'piece' (integer piece_count). All other 7 categories $\to$ unit: 'kg' (decimal declared_weight). Enforced by Zod superRefine in @chokro/shared.

2. Immutable Ledger Math: Balances are never updated in place. Calculated via apps/api/lib/LedgerMath.ts as $\sum \text{amount}$ where status = 'VERIFIED'. Flips from Pending to Verified are owned strictly by CreditVerificationDomain.ts.

3. Seam Isolation: Route handlers are thin controllers wrapped in safeRoute(). Business domains in apps/api/lib/domain/ never import @chokro/db. Repositories wrap all queries in withDb() transactions.
